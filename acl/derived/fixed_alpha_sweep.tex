\section{Intervention magnitude sweep}
\label{app:fixed-alpha-sweep}
We repeat the paired-accuracy intervention from Section~\ref{sec:intervention} at six fixed magnitudes. At every magnitude, we subtract the direction from critical contexts and add it to controls; all other settings are unchanged. Each table reports the learned causal direction and the average over ten norm-matched random directions. The $|\alpha|=5$ table gives the largest-magnitude values plotted in Figure~\ref{fig:intervention-alpha-sweep}.
